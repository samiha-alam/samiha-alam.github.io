Electrocoagulation of textile wastewater using aluminum–zinc electrodes: effect of sodium chloride electrolyte dose on treatment performance and discharge compliance

Amimul Ahsan*, Samiha Alam, Munwar Mahfuze Rakib, Alif Abedin · 3rd International Conference on Recent Innovation in Civil Engineering for Sustainable Development (IICSD-2027), DUET, Gazipur, 5–6 February 2027 · extended abstract

Abstract acceptedConference

On real effluent from an ETP in Gazipur, a NaCl dose of 1.0–1.25 g/L removed 51–59% of turbidity and 75–79% of colour while staying within the ECR 2023 limits. Higher doses removed more but took the effluent out of compliance.

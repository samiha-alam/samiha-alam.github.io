Multi-criteria ranking of textile wastewater treatment options

2025 – 2026 · Thesis analysis · Team of three — Python / Excel

Scored 120 treatment alternatives across electrocoagulation, solar photocatalysis and multimedia filtration on 13 criteria, then ranked them with TOPSIS, VIKOR and PROMETHEE II in a two-tier design: each technology on its own, then all alternatives pooled.

Result: Solar photocatalysis at 1.25 g/L TiO2 was the Tier 2 consensus winner; the recommended treatment is a hybrid of filtration, photocatalysis and low-dose electrocoagulation.

Industrial training at the Local Government Engineering Department (LGED)

Sep – Oct 2025 · Four weeks · Planning, design, estimation, quality control and field visits

Rotated through LGED's planning, design, estimation and procurement, maintenance and road safety, quality control, small-scale water resources and ICT and GIS units. Saw how projects are conceived and appraised, how road and bridge designs are developed, how tenders follow the Public Procurement Rules, and how digital systems such as RSDMS and RSEPS track maintenance and monitoring.

Field visits included the Shitolokkha Nodi Bridge in Kapasia, Gazipur, and a school and a land office at Joydebpur — pile foundations, reinforcement works and site supervision.
